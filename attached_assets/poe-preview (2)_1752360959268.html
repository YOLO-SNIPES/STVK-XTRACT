OVERVIEW

STAK-XTRACT is an AI-powered audio sample extraction and arrangement tool designed for music producers, beatmakers, and audio enthusiasts. It uses advanced signal processing algorithms to intelligently identify and extract samples from audio files.

GETTING STARTED

  1. Upload Audio: Click [UPLOAD_AUDIO.exe] to select an audio file from your device (supports MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A)
  2. Load Sample: Or click [LOAD_SAMPLE_PACK.exe] to use the provided demo audio
  3. Configure Analysis: Choose your analysis type and sensitivity level
  4. Execute Scan: Click [EXECUTE_AI_SCAN.exe] to analyze the audio
  5. Review Samples: View extracted samples in the waveform and sample grid
  6. Arrange: Drag samples to create arrangements or use AI generation
  7. Export: Download your samples and arrangements as a ZIP file

ANALYSIS TYPES

Sample Extraction (Onsets)

Identifies distinct sound events or "onsets" in the audio using energy-based detection algorithms.

Beat Detection

Analyzes the rhythmic pulse of the audio and marks detected beat events using tempo analysis.

Rhythm Analysis

Detects complex rhythmic patterns and variations in the audio timeline.

Full Analysis

Combines onset detection, beat analysis, and rhythmic pattern recognition for comprehensive breakdown.

// FREQUENTLY_ASKED_QUESTIONS.exe //

Q: What audio formats does STAK-XTRACT support?

A: STAK-XTRACT supports common audio formats including MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, and M4A.

Q: How does the AI analysis work?

A: The analysis uses advanced signal processing algorithms including onset detection, tempo analysis, and spectral analysis to identify musical events in audio. The AI arrangement feature uses machine learning to suggest creative sequences.

Q: Can I use my own audio files?

A: Absolutely! Click the "[UPLOAD_AUDIO.exe]" button to select any compatible audio file from your device.

Q: What is "Sensitivity Level" for analysis?

A: This controls the threshold for event detection. "High" sensitivity detects more events (potentially creating more, shorter samples), while "Low" is more selective.

Q: How do I save my extracted samples and arrangements?

A: Use the "[EXPORT_ZIP.exe]" button in the Export Module. It packages all extracted samples and your current arrangement as a ZIP file.

// PRIVACY_PROTOCOL.exe //

At YOLO SNIPES, your privacy is paramount. This protocol outlines how STAK-XTRACT handles your data:

Data Processing:

All audio processing and sample extraction occurs directly within your browser using Web Audio API. Your audio files are never uploaded to external servers for analysis. This ensures your sensitive audio data remains private and secure on your device.

AI Features:

The AI arrangement feature may send anonymized metadata about your samples to generate suggestions. No actual audio data is transmitted.

No Personal Data Collection:

STAK-XTRACT does not collect any personal identifying information (PII) about you or your usage patterns. We do not use cookies for tracking, nor do we store any user data.

Local Storage:

Any temporary data (like loaded audio buffers or extracted sample metadata) is stored only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session and is cleared when you close the tab.

By using STAK-XTRACT, you agree to this privacy protocol. We are committed to transparency and protecting your digital privacy.
